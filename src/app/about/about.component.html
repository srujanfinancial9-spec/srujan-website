Here’s how we started

Hi, I'm Deepali, an MBA finance graduate, and a Qualified Personal Finance Professional (QPFP®) with over 20 years of experience in the finance industry.

Having spent 15 years in the corporate world, holding significant positions at esteemed financial institutions like Franklin Templeton Investments, ICICI Prudential AMC, and Axis AMC Ltd, I couldn't ignore a glaring reality:young professionals were often overlooked and turned away by most financial planners who are suited for people with massive assets and investments.  

As someone who grew up in an average middle-class family myself, I understand how significiant it is to have access to financial guidance early on in your career to make the most out of your earning years. And that’s exactly why we started Srujan FS.   

At Srujan financial, we've helped over 250 young professionals protect their wealth, prepare for the future, and most importantly, strike their own equation with money, so that they can live a stress-free life without having to worry about money.

In addition to running Srujan financial, I have also authored a book titled "Why Greed is Great!" which delves into the captivating realm of money management in a lucid manner.

The book explores the psychology behind money-making, our distinct relationship with money, and the equilibrium we all strive to achieve, and the need for financial planning through 12 unique life stories.
