Here’s how we started

Hi, My name is Deepali and I’m a CFP with over 20 years of experience in the financial industry, and have helped over no young professionals get ready for the future, protect their wealth, and most importantly, live a stress-free life. 

After years of working in the finance Industry, including serving as a VP of Business Development for a prominent wealth management firm, I was struck by a glaring reality: young professionals were often overlooked and turned away by most financial planners who are suited for people with massive assets and investments.  

As someone who grew up in an average middle-class family, I understand how important it is to have a financial plan that fits your specific lifestyle and goals. And that’s exactly why we started Srujan FS. At Srujan, we create personalized, holistic financial plans grounded in empathy and understanding.  

So, whether you’re a young professional just starting out, or someone who is planning to retire securely , we’re here to help you achieve your goals and live your best life.
