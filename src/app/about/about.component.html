Here’s how we started

Hi, My name is Deepali and I’m a Qualified Personal Finance Professional (QPFP®) with over 20 years of experience in the finance industry. I’ve helped over 250 young professionals protect their wealth, prepare for the future, and most importantly, live a stress-free life without having to worry about money.

After years of working in the finance Industry, including serving as a VP of Business Development for a prominent wealth management firm, I was struck by a glaring reality: young professionals were often overlooked and turned away by most financial planners who are suited for people with massive assets and investments.  

As someone who grew up in an average middle-class family myself, I understand how important it is to have access to financial guidance early on in your career to make the most out of your earning years. And that’s exactly why we started Srujan FS.   

At Srujan, we provide financial guidance to help young professional like you feel in control of your finances and achieve your financial goals, and we do this through a personalized approach that’s grounded in empathy and understanding. 

So, whether you're just starting out in your career or looking to make a major financial decision, we’re here to help you achieve your goals and live your best life.
