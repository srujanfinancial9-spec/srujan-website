Nature of Services

Srujan Financial Services LLP is an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor. We help investors with mutual fund transactions such as purchases, redemptions, switches and systematic investments in the schemes of the AMCs we are empanelled with.

We are not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser. The information we share is incidental to the distribution of mutual fund products and should not be treated as investment advice under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.

Commission Disclosure

As a Mutual Fund Distributor, we are paid commission by the AMCs whose schemes we distribute. This commission is paid out of the expense ratio of the Regular Plan of a scheme. We do not charge investors any separate fee for distribution.

Commission rates differ from one AMC to another and across scheme categories, and are revised by the AMCs from time to time. The brokerage structure issued to us by each AMC is published below.

Transparency & Conflict of Interest

The schemes we suggest are chosen on the basis of the investor's goals, time horizon and risk profile.

Because we earn commission from AMCs, and the commission differs between schemes, a potential conflict of interest exists. We disclose this so that you can take it into account, and we encourage you to read the Scheme Information Document, Key Information Memorandum and Statement of Additional Information of a scheme before investing.

No Guarantees or Assured Returns

Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Read all scheme related documents carefully before investing.

We do not guarantee or assure returns, the protection of capital, or the performance of any scheme. Past performance is not indicative of future results.

Client Responsibility

Investors are responsible for reading the scheme documents, assessing their own risk appetite and making the final investment decision. Investors should also keep their KYC, bank and contact details up to date and retain records of their transactions.
